On tiers: every LANCER NPC class is built at a chosen tier, so any contact here could in principle be fielded at T2 or T3. Fourteen of them say so in their own scanned rules text — a weapon that gains attacks at T2 and T3, a Reliable value written per tier, heat written as 2/3/4. Reading this index

Two things worth knowing before you use these entries: the pairs most easily mistaken for one another, and the conventions used in the stat blocks.

Witch and Witch 2 are both real

Not a duplicate entry. Their stat lines are identical down to every number, but Witch 2 carries a fifth system, Petrify, which chains Slowed into Immobilized into Stunned.

If a Witch reaches its third round without that chain, it is the base version.

Two units called Operator

Operator is the T1 build. Security Unit 'Operator' is the same chassis at T2 — better across every defence and skill, with four extra systems and a stronger death explosion.

Two units called Hornet

Gongsi and Stormrider both field one, and they scan identically — not one of the sixteen stats differs. The tell is behavioural: the Gongsi build fights at range with Hex Missiles, the Stormrider build teleports in and plants coreworms.

Structure, Stress and Armor filled in

COMP/CON only draws the Structure and Stress track once a template pushes it past 1, so most scans show nothing there. Every NPC has at least 1 of each, so that is what this index records rather than a blank. Likewise a scan with no armor plate means Armor 0.

Heat is the exception and stays blank where absent: biologicals have no heat track at all, which is a real difference, not a gap.
